## 1. Overview
The integration of Artificial Intelligence into Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) phases fundamentally shifts software engineering from manual syntax synthesis to prompt engineering, architectural guidance, automated edge-case generation, and rigorous verification.

This document presents a structured phase-by-phase comparison between the Traditional SDLC and the AI-Driven SDLC as applied directly to the CivicLens (P50) municipal platform.

## 3. Quantitative & Qualitative Impact on CivicLens

### 3.1 Time Allocation Breakdown
- **Traditional Development Profile:** 30% Requirements/Design, 45% Coding, 20% Testing, 5% Documentation.
- **AI-Augmented Development Profile (CivicLens):** 20% Requirements/Design, 25% AI Prompting & Code Refactoring, 40% Rigorous Verification & Testing, 15% Architectural Audit & Evidence Recording.

### 3.2 Key Engineering Benefits Observed
1. **Mathematical Scaffolding:** Implementing the spherical Haversine trigonometric formula and normalized TF-IDF vectors was accomplished rapidly through targeted prompting, allowing the team to focus on numerical precision and boundary cases ($0\text{m}$ to $10\text{m}$).
2. **Resilience Engineering:** When external MongoDB instances were absent, AI assistance accelerated the authoring of an in-memory dual-mode fallback repository with identical query signatures, enabling uninterrupted automated test execution.
3. **Comprehensive Test Coverage:** AI-generated test vectors allowed expanding test cases to cover unusual coordinate anomalies (polar latitudes, longitude wrapping) and edge-case paraphrasing.

### 3.3 Critical Risks & Human-in-the-Loop Countermeasures
- **Risk 1: Algorithmic Hallucination:** The initial AI prompt for text similarity produced an IDF calculation that penalized shared words in short texts.  
  *Countermeasure:* Human engineering inspection identified the flaw and refactored the engine to use normalized Term Frequency (TF) cosine vectors.
- **Risk 2: Synthetic Bias:** Over-reliance on synthetic Bangalore ward data could mask regional dialect nuances.  
  *Countermeasure:* Introduced multilingual and informal transliteration tokens into the vocabulary matrix.
