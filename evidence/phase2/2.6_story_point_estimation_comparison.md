## 1. Overview
In accordance with Phase 2 requirements, all 20 product backlog user stories were estimated independently using two divergent estimation methodologies:
1. **Traditional Human Planning Poker** (Consensus estimation by the engineering team using Fibonacci cards).
2. **AI Estimation Agent** (LLM prompt-driven scoring evaluating technical complexity, database schema indexing, and edge-case testing effort).

This document presents the comprehensive 20-story comparative matrix, variance analysis ($\Delta$), delta rationale, and velocity calibration across the two official one-week sprints and post-sprint milestones.
