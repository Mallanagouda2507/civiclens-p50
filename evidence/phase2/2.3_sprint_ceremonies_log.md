## 2. Official Sprint 1 (Week 1): Architecture, Identity & Geospatial Scaffolding
- **Sprint Duration:** 7 Calendar Days (Week 1)
- **Sprint Goal:** Establish repository monorepo, JWT RBAC authentication, dual-mode data persistence, and great-circle Haversine coordinate math.
- **Committed Backlog:** US-01 (3), US-02 (2), US-03 (3), US-04 (3), US-05 (3), US-09 (2), US-10 (3) = **19 Story Points**.

### 2.1 Sprint 1 Planning Ceremony (Day 1)
- **Backlog Selection:** Team pulled US-01 through US-05, US-09, and US-10 based on Definition of Ready (DoR).
- **Technical Commitment:** Decided to implement `dataStore.js` with dual-mode fallback (MongoDB Mongoose + in-memory repository) to ensure zero dependency blockers during automated CI and offline evaluation.

### 2.2 Sprint 1 Daily Standups (Sample Minutes)
- **Day 3 Standup:**
  - *Yesterday:* Implemented bcrypt hashing (10 salt rounds) and JWT signing in `authController.js`.
  - *Today:* Connecting Express `protect` and `authorize` middleware to route handlers.
  - *Impediment:* Supertest port binding on Windows. Resolved by configuring loopback permissions.
- **Day 5 Standup:**
  - *Yesterday:* Completed Haversine geodesic math in `geoSpatialService.js`.
  - *Today:* Writing bounds checking tests for polar coordinates ($\pm 90^\circ, \pm 180^\circ$).
  - *Impediment:* None.

### 2.3 Sprint 1 Review & Demonstration (Day 7)
- **Deliverables Demonstrated:**
  1. Live HTTP registration and login with bcrypt hashing and JWT bearer token issuance.
  2. Role-gated middleware: HTTP 403 Forbidden correctly returned when citizen attempts staff route.
  3. Haversine distance calculations verified against known geographic coordinates.
  4. Passing test suite: 22 tests passing with 100% pass rate.
- **Stakeholder / Academic Feedback:** The dual-mode store was highly praised for decoupling external MongoDB dependencies.

### 2.4 Sprint 1 Retrospective (Day 7)
- **What Went Well:** AI prompt assistance accelerated boilerplate Express controllers by over 50%.
- **What Could Be Improved:** Initial AI-generated Mongoose schemas included deprecated options (`useNewUrlParser`).
- **Action Item:** Standardize schema prompts to target Mongoose v8+ clean syntax.

## 3. Official Sprint 2 (Week 2): AI NLP Engines, Duplicate Clustering & Lifecycle
- **Sprint Duration:** 7 Calendar Days (Week 2)
- **Sprint Goal:** Deliver NLP text similarity, automated duplicate clustering, 6-class department routing, complaint state transitions, and staff triage interface.
- **Committed Backlog:** US-07 (3), US-11 (3), US-12 (2), US-13 (2), US-14 (3), US-15 (3), US-17 (3) = **18 Story Points**.

### 3.1 Sprint 2 Planning Ceremony (Day 8)
- **Backlog Selection:** Team pulled US-07, US-11 to US-15, and US-17 to complete the complaint lifecycle and duplicate clustering.
- **Technical Commitment:** Replace heavy Python transformer microservices with native Node.js TF-IDF Cosine + Jaccard similarity to eliminate inter-process latency.

### 3.2 Sprint 2 Daily Standups (Sample Minutes)
- **Day 10 Standup:**
  - *Yesterday:* Tested text similarity. Discovered mathematical bug where standard 2-doc IDF formula zeroed out cosine similarity for shared words.
  - *Today:* Refactored `textSimilarityService.js` to use normalized Term Frequency (TF) cosine vectors.
  - *Impediment:* Paraphrased pothole reports initially scored $0.32$. After TF refactor, score elevated to $0.73$.
- **Day 12 Standup:**
  - *Yesterday:* Integrated state machine: `SUBMITTED` $\rightarrow$ `ASSIGNED` $\rightarrow$ `IN_PROGRESS` $\rightarrow$ `RESOLVED`.
  - *Today:* Enforcing mandatory resolution remarks in `complaintController.js` upon status closure.
  - *Impediment:* None.

### 3.3 Sprint 2 Review & Demonstration (Day 14)
- **Deliverables Demonstrated:**
  1. Real-time duplicate clustering: Incoming complaint within 25 meters tagged as duplicate, linked to master ticket, incrementing `duplicateCount`.
  2. 6-class department routing with explainable keywords (`#pothol`, `#asphalt`).
  3. Complete complaint lifecycle with immutable append-only audit trail.
  4. Passing test suite expanded to 41 automated tests.
- **Stakeholder / Academic Feedback:** Explainable AI (XAI) keywords provide great transparency for municipal officers.

### 3.4 Sprint 2 Retrospective (Day 14)
- **What Went Well:** The two-stage spatial pruning filter ($75\text{m}$ Haversine cutoff) reduced candidate comparisons from $O(N^2)$ to $O(k)$, running in $< 8\text{ms}$.
- **What Could Be Improved:** Leaflet map container height collapsed on initial React mount.
- **Action Item:** Added explicit CSS min-height rules and `invalidateSize` trigger on component mount.
