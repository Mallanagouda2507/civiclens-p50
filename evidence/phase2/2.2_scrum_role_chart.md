## 4. Role Descriptions & AI Collaboration Touchpoints

### 4.1 Product Owner (PO)
- **Primary Mandate:** Maximize product value; define citizen and municipal user stories; maintain INVEST backlog prioritization.
- **AI Collaboration Touchpoint:** Used conversational LLMs to generate realistic municipal complaint edge cases (e.g., informal colloquialisms, cross-ward boundaries).
- **Core Artifacts Owned:** Product Backlog, Definition of Ready (DoR), Sprint Goals.

### 4.2 Scrum Master (SM)
- **Primary Mandate:** Ensure adherence to Scrum practices; remove blockers; protect team from scope creep; uphold the Definition of Done.
- **AI Collaboration Touchpoint:** Evaluated variance between human Planning Poker and AI Story Point estimates to detect under-estimated algorithmic risks.
- **Core Artifacts Owned:** Sprint Burndown / Velocity Tracking, Retrospective Action Item Log, Impediment Log.

### 4.3 Development Team
- **Primary Mandate:** Deliver potentially shippable product increments meeting the Definition of Done at the end of each sprint.
- **Specializations:** Full-stack React/Node.js, geospatial algorithms, automated testing, containerization.
- **AI Collaboration Touchpoint:** Used AI for boilerplate synthesis, algorithmic translation (Haversine geodesic math), and edge-case unit test drafting, followed by rigorous human verification.

### 4.4 Municipal Stakeholders
- **Primary Mandate:** Validate real-world applicability of department routing, SLA resolution timelines, and geographic privacy boundaries.
- **Touchpoint:** Participated in end-of-sprint reviews to evaluate map usability and triage dashboard workflows.
