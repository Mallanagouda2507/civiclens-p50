## 1. Overview
In accordance with Phase 4 academic requirements, this document presents an empirical comparative analysis of **15 core test cases** evaluated under two distinct execution paradigms:
1. **Manual Testing:** Human tester performing manual Postman clicks, manual form filling in browser, and spreadsheet tracking.
2. **Automated Testing:** Programmatic execution via Jest Supertest and headless Selenium WebDriver.

## 3. Quantitative Analysis & Return on Investment (ROI)

- **Manual Execution Time:** 15.4 minutes for a single test run.
- **Automated Execution Time:** 0.22 seconds (Jest Supertest runner completes all 15 tests in less than a quarter of a second).
- **Speedup Factor:**
  $$\text{Speedup} = \frac{T_{\text{manual}}}{T_{\text{automated}}} = \frac{925\text{ s}}{0.218\text{ s}} \approx 4,243\times$$
- **Regression Compounding:** Across 40 Git commits during development:
  - Cumulative manual testing effort would have consumed: $40 \times 15.4\text{ mins} = 616\text{ mins} \approx 10.3\text{ hours}$.
  - Cumulative automated testing effort consumed: $40 \times 0.22\text{ s} \approx 8.8\text{ seconds}$.
- **Accuracy & Subjectivity Mitigation:** In TC-10 and TC-11 (text similarity), manual human evaluation is vulnerable to subjective bias (e.g., whether "crater" is similar to "pothole"). Automated TF-IDF vectorization provides 100% repeatable mathematical determination.
