## 2. Gherkin Acceptance Criteria Verification Matrix

### Epic 1: Identity & Role-Based Access Control
- **UAT-01: Citizen Self-Registration & Authentication**
  - *Scenario:* Successful citizen registration with valid credentials.
  - *Given* an unauthenticated visitor navigates to the registration view.
  - *When* they submit a valid name, unique email, and password $\ge 6$ characters.
  - *Then* the password is hashed with bcrypt ($\ge 10$ salt rounds), a 24h JWT token is signed, and the role is set to `citizen`.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in `tests/auth.test.js`).

- **UAT-02: Role-Gated Staff Triage Protection**
  - *Scenario:* Enforcing role-based authorization for municipal triage endpoints.
  - *Given* an authenticated user with role `citizen`.
  - *When* they attempt a PUT request to update status at `/api/complaints/:id/status`.
  - *Then* the system rejects access with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in `tests/auth.test.js`).

### Epic 2: Geotagged Intake & Interactive Leaflet GIS
- **UAT-03: Coordinate Capture via Map Pin-Drop**
  - *Scenario:* Capturing geotagged coordinates on complaint submission.
  - *Given* a citizen on the complaint submission form.
  - *When* they click on the interactive Leaflet map picker.
  - *Then* marker coordinates `[longitude, latitude]` are validated within boundary limits and attached to the complaint payload.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in Selenium E2E Action 2 & 3).

- **UAT-04: Civic Map GIS Pin Visualization**
  - *Scenario:* Visualizing active complaints on the interactive map.
  - *Given* active complaints stored in the municipal database.
  - *When* a user opens the Civic Map view.
  - *Then* complaints render as color-coded pins (Red: Open, Amber: In Progress, Green: Resolved) with interactive popups showing tracking IDs.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in Selenium E2E Action 5).

### Epic 3: AI Spatial-Textual Duplicate Clustering
- **UAT-05: Real-Time Duplicate Detection and Endorsement**
  - *Scenario:* Identifying a nearby paraphrased complaint as duplicate.
  - *Given* an existing complaint at `[77.6412, 12.9784]` ("Deep road pothole").
  - *When* a citizen files a complaint within 75 meters ("Broken asphalt crater near KFC") with similarity $\ge 0.60$.
  - *Then* the system tags the incoming complaint as a duplicate, links it to the master ticket, and increments `duplicateCount`.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in `tests/complaints.test.js` & `tests/aiEngine.test.js`).

### Epic 4: Automated Department Routing
- **UAT-06: NLP Multi-Class Department Routing**
  - *Scenario:* Auto-routing a water pipeline leak complaint.
  - *Given* an incoming complaint with description "Burst drinking water pipeline leaking on road".
  - *When* the AI Department Classifier evaluates the text.
  - *Then* it assigns `WATER_SUPPLY` with confidence $\ge 0.60$ and returns explainability keywords `["pipe", "water", "leak", "burst"]`.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in `tests/aiEngine.test.js`).

### Epic 5: Lifecycle Tracking & Municipal Analytics
- **UAT-07: Public Tracking & Audit History Verification**
  - *Scenario:* Citizen checking status using tracking reference ID.
  - *Given* a filed complaint with tracking ID `CL-2026-D55057`.
  - *When* the citizen queries the tracking endpoint.
  - *Then* the full lifecycle timeline, department assignment, and official resolution remarks are returned.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in Selenium E2E Action 4).

- **UAT-08: Executive Municipal Telemetry & Analytics**
  - *Scenario:* Municipal Commissioner reviewing department resolution metrics.
  - *Given* complaints distributed across multiple municipal departments.
  - *When* the analytics summary endpoint is queried.
  - *Then* total count, resolution percentage, duplicate filtration savings, and average SLA hours are calculated.
  - *UAT Result:* **VERIFIED PASS** (Tested in `tests/complaints.test.js`).
